Cooperation focus

Production work that connects the website to real operations.

Development & Support is useful when the project needs reliable implementation, responsive pages, form handling, technical setup, launch checks, and a clear path for updates after release.

Helsinka Studio keeps the build practical: the website should be stable, understandable, editable where needed, and ready for campaign activity.

Services included

What can be included in this direction

Website Development

Production implementation of responsive pages, forms, content structures, SEO basics, and launch-ready website behavior.

Problem it solves
Approved designs or page plans need to become a stable, working website that can support real inquiries and campaigns.
What we do
Build responsive layouts, structure reusable sections, connect forms, prepare metadata basics, check performance, and support deployment.
Deliverables
Production website pages, responsive QA, form setup, basic SEO structure, launch checklist, and deployment support.
Client outcome
You get a working website that is ready for launch, campaign traffic, and future updates.

Frontend Development

Interface implementation focused on responsive behavior, clean HTML/CSS/JavaScript, usability, and maintainable components.

Problem it solves
The visual design exists, but the interface needs to be coded accurately, responsively, and without unnecessary technical weight.
What we do
Build layouts, interactions, responsive states, navigation behavior, reusable UI sections, accessibility basics, and browser/device checks.
Deliverables
Frontend code, responsive components, interaction behavior, accessibility notes, QA fixes, and implementation documentation.
Client outcome
The website feels consistent across devices and is easier to maintain after launch.

Backend Development

Server-side support for forms, validation, notifications, integrations, content handling, and practical website workflows.

Problem it solves
The website needs more than static pages: inquiries must be processed, validated, routed, or connected to internal tools.
What we do
Plan backend requirements, build form handling, sanitize submissions, configure email notifications, connect simple integrations, and document workflows.
Deliverables
Backend form handler, validation logic, notification setup, integration notes, data-flow documentation, and testing checklist.
Client outcome
Your website can reliably collect project inquiries and support operational workflows after launch.

Email Marketing Systems

Lead capture and email workflow planning for inquiries, campaigns, downloads, events, or follow-up communication.

Problem it solves
Leads arrive through forms, but follow-up is manual, inconsistent, or disconnected from marketing activity.
What we do
Plan form-to-list logic, define segmentation, outline email templates, recommend automation steps, and connect lead capture with page goals.
Deliverables
List structure, signup flow, email template outline, segmentation notes, automation recommendations, and handoff documentation.
Client outcome
New contacts can move into a clearer communication flow instead of disappearing after form submission.

Website Support & Maintenance

Ongoing support for content updates, technical checks, small improvements, campaign additions, and post-launch stability.

Problem it solves
The website launches, but the team still needs updates, campaign pages, checks, fixes, and improvements over time.
What we do
Handle content changes, review forms, add campaign sections, monitor small issues, update page content, and plan incremental improvements.
Deliverables
Maintenance workflow, update queue, form checks, content updates, campaign page support, and improvement notes.
Client outcome
The website stays useful after launch and can support new business activity without becoming stale.

FAQ

Development and support questions

Can development start from existing designs?

Yes. Existing designs can be reviewed and prepared for production before implementation starts.

Can this include PHP form handling?

Yes. Form processing, validation, email notifications, consent fields, and anti-spam logic can be included.

Can support continue after launch?

Yes. Maintenance, campaign pages, small improvements, and content updates can continue as an ongoing support process.
