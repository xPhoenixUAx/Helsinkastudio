Cooperation focus

A practical planning layer for websites, campaigns, and launches.

Strategy & Planning is useful when the team knows it needs better digital work, but the scope, offer, target audience, and campaign path are still unclear.

Helsinka Studio turns that uncertainty into a structured plan that can guide content, design, development, advertising, analytics, and future support.

Services included

What can be included in this direction

Marketing Strategy

A working digital plan that defines who the project is for, what the offer should communicate, and which channels deserve attention first.

Problem it solves
The team wants a new website or campaign, but the audience, message, offer, and priorities are still too vague for production.
What we do
Review the current business context, clarify target segments, map decision triggers, define positioning, and create a message framework for pages and campaigns.
Deliverables
Audience map, offer structure, messaging framework, channel priorities, launch recommendations, and notes for design and content teams.
Client outcome
You know what needs to be built, why it matters, and how the website and campaigns should support business goals.

Performance Marketing

Campaign planning that connects paid traffic with landing pages, conversion actions, tracking, and lead quality review.

Problem it solves
Advertising spend is planned before the website, landing pages, and reporting are ready to support it.
What we do
Structure campaign goals, audience segments, conversion paths, landing page needs, tracking events, and a review rhythm for performance decisions.
Deliverables
Campaign structure, funnel map, landing page recommendations, conversion tracking brief, reporting cadence, and improvement priorities.
Client outcome
Paid campaigns launch with clearer expectations and a practical way to judge what should be changed next.

Paid Social Campaigns

A planning layer for social campaigns that aligns audiences, creative angles, landing pages, and inquiry flow.

Problem it solves
Social ads attract attention but do not connect cleanly to a page, offer, form, or follow-up process.
What we do
Define audience groups, message angles, creative directions, ad variations, landing page roles, and the next step after a visitor clicks.
Deliverables
Audience plan, campaign concept map, creative brief, ad message variants, page recommendations, and tracking requirements.
Client outcome
Social campaign work becomes easier to produce, launch, and evaluate because the message and page path are connected.

Search Advertising

Search campaign structure based on real intent, service-page relevance, landing page readiness, and conversion tracking.

Problem it solves
The business wants search traffic, but pages are not mapped to search intent or ready to answer buying questions.
What we do
Group search intent, map keywords to pages, identify landing page gaps, plan ad groups, and define conversion checks for inquiries.
Deliverables
Keyword grouping, ad group structure, landing page map, content gap notes, conversion checklist, and reporting recommendations.
Client outcome
Search traffic is directed toward pages that are more relevant, easier to measure, and more useful for qualified inquiries.

Analytics & Reporting

A measurement setup plan that helps the team understand which pages, campaigns, and forms create useful business signals.

Problem it solves
The website may receive traffic or inquiries, but the team cannot clearly see what is working or where leads come from.
What we do
Review analytics needs, define key events, map form conversions, outline source reporting, and prepare a simple performance review structure.
Deliverables
Measurement plan, event checklist, conversion points, reporting structure, dashboard recommendations, and post-launch review notes.
Client outcome
You get a practical basis for improving pages, campaigns, and inquiry quality after launch instead of guessing.

FAQ

Strategy questions before cooperation starts

Can we start here if we do not have a clear brief?

Yes. This direction is designed for early-stage clarity, especially when the project needs structure before production.

Does this include advertising management?

It can include campaign planning and setup recommendations. Ongoing advertising management can be scoped separately if needed.

Can this direction lead into design and development?

Yes. The output is meant to guide the next stages: content, web design, development, launch, and support.
